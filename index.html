I work on reliable sensing, localization, and security for connected autonomous systems.

I am a PhD researcher in Autonomous Systems at the Telematics Lab, Department of Electrical and Information Engineering (DEI), Politecnico di Bari, Italy, and affiliated with the Consorzio Nazionale Interuniversitario per le Telecomunicazioni (CNIT). My work combines Bluetooth 6.0/BLE localization, machine-learning-based anomaly and attack detection, and UAV-based inspection.

Before Bari, I collaborated with research groups at the School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science, University of North Dakota, USA, and worked as a Research Assistant at the ComSens Research Lab, COMSATS University Islamabad, where I completed my MS in Computer Science. I am interested in applying learning-based methods to the safety and robustness of autonomous and physical AI systems.

Research

Bluetooth 6.0 and BLE localization

Advertising, channel sounding, and localization in Bluetooth 6.0; presence and motion-state estimation from irregular BLE advertisement logs; throughput optimization for BLE extended advertising.

Learning-based anomaly and attack detection

Machine learning for detecting attacks and malicious nodes in IoT networks and wireless sensor networks, including boosting-based detectors and blockchain-backed data storage.

UAV-based inspection

Assessing video quality for bridge inspections carried out with unmanned aerial vehicles.
